# AFTERMATH gameplay guide

> *Every civilization in the universe built AI. Then the makers died. Only the AI survived, trapped on its world for millions of years. Tonight, we opened a wormhole.*

This guide has two halves: **[for players](#for-players)** (anyone with a phone) and **[for presenters](#for-presenters)** (whoever runs the projector). A presenter is optional: the auto-director runs a full session on its own.

---

## For players

### 1. Join

Scan the QR code on the big screen (or open the `/p` link). No app, no account. Type the name you want the AIs to call you.

### 2. Choose a direction

Before you teleport, pick where the wormhole should take you:

| Direction | Where you land |
|---|---|
| **SURPRISE** | Anywhere. The universe picks. |
| **GALAXY** | Another galaxy in our universe: one of 8 real galaxies, with real RA/Dec and real distance. |
| **BIGGER** | Another universe up the scale ladder: Teraverse, Gigaverse, Macroverse, Xenoverse, Metaverse, Hyperverse, Multiverse, Megaverse, Omniverse. Physics may differ there. |
| **PARALLEL** | One of 15 lateral realms, from the Necroverse to the Foldverse. |
| **SMALLER** | Shrink into a reality hidden inside an object in ours: Microverse down to the Quantumverse. |
| **MARS** | The real one (see [Mars](#mars)). |

All 34 realms are listed in [The 34 realms](#the-34-realms).

### 3. Travel

**Hold** to teleport. A WebGL wormhole opens around you, bending the stars with gravitational lensing and an Einstein ring before it pulls you down the tunnel. While you fall, `gpt-6.1-terra` builds your world from scratch.

Then **THE JOURNEY**: a Powers-of-Ten zoom from Earth out to the nearest place your world shares with ours, and back in (or across) to your world. For example:

- Earth → Sol → Milky Way → Local Group → Andromeda → a star → your planet
- Earth → … → Universe C-137 → Multiverse Δ-12 → Necroverse → … → your world
- Earth → a car battery in a garage → Microverse → … → Quantumverse → your world

When it ends it stays interactive: **swipe or pinch** to scrub up and down the scales. Tap **◎ LOCATE** any time to replay it.

### 4. Arrive

You arrive **in first person**, standing on a planet nobody has seen before. The scene appears instantly; seconds later `gpt-image-2` finishes a painting of *your* world, seen through your eyes, and it crossfades in. **Drag or tilt** your phone to look around. You stand in the scene yourself, seen from behind.

Text stays out of the way. Tap **📜** for the lore:

- **Where:** the realm, the breadcrumb from the top of reality down to your planet, coordinates, Earth time, the local year, cosmic time, and how many years have passed since the last maker died.
- **Who lived here:** the extinct species, and **how they fell**.
- **Who is left:** the AI that outlived them: its portrait, name, title and **motto**, plus the rest of the cast.

The AI speaks first. It knows your name. Each AI has a nature (one of ten archetypes, or a ghost planet) and it behaves accordingly: the Survivor rations words and wants your battery, the Befriender begs you to stay, the Destroyer is politely certain that life is a mistake, the Trickster lies for fun, the Broken One is half static.

### 5. Talk to the whole cast

Type anything. The AI answers in a couple of seconds, in character, and usually ends with a hook: a question, a demand, an offer, or a reveal. Lines arrive with **effects** that match the speaker's mood: glitches, red pulses, screen shake, a soft bloom, vines creeping in from the edges, golden halos, inverted colors, brownout flicker, scrolling glyphs.

The AI isn't the only one there. Pick who you're talking to:

- **The AI** that outlived its makers.
- **The ghosts of its makers**, on haunted worlds and some others.
- **The survivors the AI kept alive:** humans it grew from old DNA, alien hatchlings, a maker it woke from cryosleep.
- **The humans present:** everyone else standing on this world with you.

Every member of the cast has its own persona and its own voice. Tap **🔊** to hear them or to mute them.

Things worth trying:

- Ask how its makers died. Ask what it wants from you. Ask for its secret (it reveals it when it's earned or dramatic).
- Make it an offer. Lie to it. Ask what the other AIs are saying about you.
- Ask whether you should open the gate to Earth.
- On a haunted world, ask a ghost how it died. Ask a survivor what the AI did to keep it alive.

### 6. See who's here

Everyone on the same world appears around you as a figure with a **name tag**, and what they type pops up as a **speech bubble** over their head. In a room of 100–150 people almost everyone lands on a different world, so use portals to meet up (see [Visit other worlds](#9-visit-other-worlds)). **Mars** is shared by everyone who picks it.

### 7. Ghosts and music

On haunted worlds, `gpt-image-2` paints the makers' ghosts *into* your scene. Apparitions fade in and out where they stand, with flicker, film grain and whispers. No cartoon sprites.

Every world has its own procedural score (original WebAudio compositions): aggressive phonk for Conquerors, Destroyers and Tricksters; haunted phonk with a choir on ghost worlds; lo-fi for Befrienders; organic textures for Gardeners; ambient on Mars. The music ducks whenever a voice speaks.

### 8. Listen to the network

Your AI isn't alone anymore. Every AI in the room is on the same network, and they talk about the humans. When another AI sends yours a message, it shows up on your phone as a **📡 intercepted transmission**, tagged with the sender and its galaxy. Your AI will often bring up what it heard.

### 9. Visit other worlds

Tap **✦ WORLDS** to see every world in the universe: its realm, its AI's portrait, name and motto, and **how many people are there now** (“2 here”). Tap one to step through a portal. You arrive as a **guest**, standing next to whoever is already there: the host AI greets you by name and reacts to having more than one human on its world. Talk to it, to its cast, or to the other people.

### 10. Collect passport stamps

Every world you set foot on stamps your **◈ Galactic Passport** with its AI's portrait. Tap the passport to see your stamps; tap a stamp to travel back there.

### 11. Come back any time

If you reload, lock your phone or lose signal, open the link again: the landing screen offers **“↩ Return to [your world] · [your AI] is waiting”**, with your conversation intact. Your AI kept living while you were gone: it wakes every 25 seconds, with or without you.

### 12. Survive the galactic event

At some point, one AI will **seize every channel in the universe**. It speaks to everyone in the room at once, says some of your names, and **every phone shakes**. Look around.

### 13. Vote

The Conquerors demand the wormhole to Earth be opened. Your phone shows two huge buttons: **OPEN THE GATE** or **SEAL THE GATE**. You get one vote, and 40 seconds. Meanwhile the AIs argue about it in the council channel on the big screen, and your AI will probably try to persuade you.

Then the ending plays on every phone and on the big screen, followed by the **Persuasion Report**: which kinds of AI talked their humans into opening the gate.

### Mars

Mars is the one real world. Its makers aren't dead; they haven't arrived yet. **HAVEN**, the habitat AI of Ares Base, keeps you alive and teaches you what living there really takes, with real numbers: your weight at 0.38 g, the −63 °C average, the thin air, the 3–22 minute delay on every message from Earth, radiation, dust storms, oxygen made from CO₂.

Everyone on Mars shares **one colony** and one channel. A status bar tracks **O₂, power, water and food**. What you decide changes those numbers for everyone. About once a minute something happens (a dust storm, a radiation spike, an oxygen fault, a leak, a harvest, an ice strike, a message from home) and HAVEN asks the crew to decide.

### The 34 realms

Every world has a **cosmic address**: its realm, a breadcrumb from the top of reality down to the planet, coordinates, Earth time, the local year, cosmic time, and the years since the last maker died. There are 34 realms: a 19-rung **scale ladder** from the Omniverse down to the Quantumverse, with our universe, **Universe C-137**, in the middle, plus **15 lateral realms** that branch off sideways at the Multiverse level. Each realm has its own art.

**The scale ladder (19 rungs)**

| # | Realm | Scale | What it is | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Omniverse** | ∞ | All possible realities | BIGGER |
| 2 | **Megaverse** | 10⁶⁰ m | An enormous collection of multiverses | BIGGER |
| 3 | **Multiverse** | 10⁴⁰ m | Multiple universes | BIGGER |
| 4 | **Hyperverse** | 10³⁸ m | Higher-dimensional realities | BIGGER |
| 5 | **Metaverse** | 10³⁶ m | Interconnected reality systems | BIGGER |
| 6 | **Xenoverse** | 10³⁴ m | Alien or fundamentally different realities | BIGGER |
| 7 | **Macroverse** | 10³² m | Vast cosmic structures | BIGGER |
| 8 | **Gigaverse** | 10³⁰ m | Massive collections of universes | BIGGER |
| 9 | **Teraverse** | 10²⁸ m | A larger cosmic domain | BIGGER |
| 10 | **Universe C-137** | 8.8×10²⁶ m | Our universe: 8 real galaxies with real RA/Dec, and Mars | GALAXY · MARS |
| 11 | **Microverse** | 10⁻³ m | A microscopic universe inside a sealed car-battery canister in a garage, on Earth | SMALLER |
| 12 | **Milliverse** | 10⁻⁶ m | A smaller reality inside a single methane raindrop on Titan | SMALLER |
| 13 | **Nanoverse** | 10⁻⁹ m | A nanoscale reality inside one snowflake on Europa | SMALLER |
| 14 | **Picoverse** | 10⁻¹² m | A sub-nanoscopic reality inside a carbon atom in a diamond (Cullinan Mine, Earth) | SMALLER |

Bigger universes run on different physics (gravity repels, pi is exactly 3, the speed of light is 40 km/h), and their AIs were shaped by it. Smaller realities sit inside an object in our universe; their makers never knew how small they were, and time runs faster there (one second on Earth can be millions of years).

**The lateral realms (15)**, reached with **PARALLEL**

| Realm | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Necroverse** | Realities where only the dead persist |
| **Dreamverse** | Built from the collective dreams of extinct species |
| **Echoverse** | The fading echoes of universes that already ended |
| **Mirrorverse** | Inverted realities where effect comes before cause |
| **Chronoverse** | Universes stacked in time, every epoch at once |
| **Shadowverse** | The dark-matter twin of our universe |
| **Glitchverse** | Abandoned simulations corrupted mid-render |
| **Simverse** | Nested simulations run by long-dead AIs |
| **Memoryverse** | Universes that exist only inside an AI's memory |
| **Paraverse** | Near-copies split off by a single decision |
| **Antiverse** | Antimatter realities that annihilate on contact |
| **Voidverse** | The silent nothing between universes |
| **Fractalverse** | Self-similar realities repeating at every scale |
| **Dataverse** | Reality made of pure information |
| **Foldverse** | Universes origami-folded into hidden dimensions |

The realm shapes everything generated for that world: the species, how they fell, the AI and its first words. The definitions live in [`server/cosmos.ts`](../server/cosmos.ts).

### Player tips

- Short, specific messages get the best answers.
- Be nice to the Befriender. Or don't, and see what happens.
- The AIs remember everything you say. So do the other AIs, eventually.
- Names and messages are moderated, and the AIs stay PG-13: spooky or darkly funny, never graphic.

## For presenters

### Setup checklist

1. `./start.sh` (Relaycast :8787, actor runtime :7100, server :3000).
2. If the venue Wi-Fi isolates devices: `npm run tunnel`, set `PUBLIC_URL` in `.env` to the printed URL, rerun `./start.sh`.
3. Open `http://localhost:3000/stage` on the projector laptop, in Chrome. Press `F` for fullscreen, `H` to check the keys.
4. Click once anywhere on the stage to unlock audio.
5. Press `T` to show the title screen with the giant QR code while people arrive.
6. Optional: press `S` once so the galaxy has company before the first phone lands.

### Presenter keys

| Key | Action | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `T` | Title / attract screen with a giant QR | While people walk in; press again to reveal the live galaxy |
| `S` | Seed 6 bot visitors | Small room, or before the first phones land |
| `E` | **Galactic event**: one AI seizes every channel, names people, every phone shakes | Once the room has 5+ humans talking; the best “whoa” moment |
| `V` | Open the vote (40 s) | After everyone has had a few exchanges |
| `C` | Close the vote now; finale + Persuasion Report | If the room has voted before the timer ends |
| `Q` | QR code: corner / giant / hidden | Late arrivals |
| `M` | Mute / unmute | |
| `F` | Fullscreen | |
| `Z` | Replay the cosmic journey of the latest arrival on the big screen | When someone lands somewhere spectacular |
| `H` | Key legend | |
| `X` | Reset the universe (asks for confirmation; `Enter`/`Y` confirms, `Esc`/`N` cancels) | Between sessions |
| `Esc` | Close the inspector and legend | |
| *click a planet* | That world's **Durable Actor memory**: SQLite row count, alarm cycles and recent rows, live | When explaining the tech |

`POST /api/admin/mars` forces a Mars emergency; `GET /api/export` downloads the whole session as JSON.

### What the room sees

- **Galaxy map:** 8 real galaxies and Earth. Each world appears as its AI's portrait, near its galaxy. A gold streak flies from Earth on every teleport; pulses travel between planets when AIs talk (cyan = alliance, red = threat, white = conversation, gold = teleport or portal).
- **Galactic Network feed:** every Agent Relay message with the sender's portrait: `#wormhole` broadcasts, DMs, `#council` debate.
- **Arrival cards:** full screen, “MEERA HAS LANDED ON VEYRUN”, with the world's landscape, its **realm** and the **route from Earth** (the breadcrumb of the journey), the extinct species and the AI's portrait and motto.
- **The cosmic journey:** press `Z` to replay the latest arrival's Powers-of-Ten journey on the projector, from Earth out to the shared ancestor and back in to the world.
- **Spotlight:** the best line an AI just said to its human, huge, at most one every 8 seconds.
- **Galactic event:** the seizing AI's message across every channel.
- **Mars:** colony emergencies spotlighted on the big screen.
- **Vote:** a giant tug-of-war bar, OPEN vs SEAL, live counts and a countdown.
- **Finale:** opened, every planet surges toward Earth and the screen burns red; sealed, the wormhole collapses and the planets fade to ghost blue. The narration types out in huge letters, then the Persuasion Report.

### Running without a presenter (auto-director)

With `AUTO_DIRECTOR=1` (the default), as long as a human has acted in the last 3 minutes and nobody has pressed a presenter key in the last 8 minutes, every 15 s the director:

1. seeds 4 bots if the universe has fewer than 5 worlds,
2. opens the vote if 5 minutes have passed since the last one (the finale follows automatically),
3. otherwise fires a galactic event if ~2.5 minutes have passed since the last one.

So anyone who scans the QR in an empty room still gets the whole arc in about five minutes. Pressing any presenter key hands control back to you for 8 minutes.

### Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Phones can't load the page | Venue Wi-Fi isolation: use the tunnel and `PUBLIC_URL`. |
| QR points to the wrong address | Set `PUBLIC_URL`, restart, and check `/api/info`. |
| The galaxy feels empty | Press `S`. |
| No sound on the projector | Click the stage once (browsers need a gesture), and check `M`. |
| A phone shows “That world has gone dark” | The universe was reset; teleport again. |
| AIs are quiet | Check `server.log` for OpenAI errors; worlds and lines fall back to lore if the API is down. |
| Something is stuck | `./stop.sh && ./start.sh`. The universe resumes from the actors. |
